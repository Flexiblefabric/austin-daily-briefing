Corrections log.

When we confirm a material error or a meaningful clarification in a briefing, we will note the change in the next available briefing and record it here.

No confirmed corrections yet.

How this works

We review reports of possible errors and requests for clarification. A submission does not automatically mean a correction will be made. When a change is confirmed, the notice will identify the affected briefing, explain what changed, and point to supporting information when available. We will not publish the submitter’s email address or private message.

General feedback and unconfirmed reports will not appear in this log. To report an issue, use the Feedback & corrections link in the footer.
