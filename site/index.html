AUSTIN · FOOD & CLIMATE · SEPTEMBER 1, 2026

34 local projects receive food-and-climate grants

Austin awarded $102,000 in Food and Climate Equity grants to 34 community-led projects, with up to $3,000 per organization. The projects range from a community garden aimed at healthy food access to emergency-preparedness work and school sustainability programs.

Why It Matters

These are small grants, not a citywide fix. They put funding directly into local efforts on food access and resilience. The City says recipients have until June 1, 2027, to complete their projects.
