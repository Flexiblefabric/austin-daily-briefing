Good to know.

What am I signing up for?

A daily email with Austin news and context, things to do, and a personalized “More for You” section. Your signup creates a personal briefing profile with sensible default settings.

How does personalization work?

You begin with default preferences. Use Customize Briefing to choose your interests and reading style. Leave questions blank to keep the settings you already have. Your choices shape “More for You”; they do not remove essential stories from the core Austin briefing.

How is my information used?

Your email address and preferences are used only to deliver and manage Austin Daily Briefing. They are not sold or shared for advertising. This landing page does not collect your email directly.

Can I stop or change my emails?

Yes. Use Manage Subscription to pause, resume, or unsubscribe. You can also reset your topic preferences. Customization and management links are available below and in every briefing.
