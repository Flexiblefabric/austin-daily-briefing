Terms, in human language.

We do not promise that every briefing will arrive at a particular time or that the service will always be available.

Editorial process and AI

Automated tools, including AI, may help discover, rank, organize, summarize, and personalize material. Editorial rules and human judgment may also shape what appears. The balance between automation and human review may change while the system is tested.

Automated summaries can misunderstand a source, omit context, or make other errors. Links are provided so readers can review original reporting and official information.
